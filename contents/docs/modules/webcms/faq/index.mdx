## Overview

The **FAQ** page displays all frequently asked questions available on the website, along with their category, answer, and display order. Administrators can review and manage FAQs from this page.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open WebCMS">
    From the main menu, click **WebCMS**, then select **FAQ**.

    ![FAQ Menu](/modules/cms-management/cms-faq-menu.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="View FAQ List">
    The FAQ page will display all questions with their Category, Question, Answer, and Order.

    ![FAQ List](/modules/cms-management/cms-faq-list.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- All FAQs are listed for the administrator to review and manage.
