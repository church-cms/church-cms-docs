## Overview

The **Add FAQ** page allows administrators to create a new question and answer to publish on the website's FAQ page.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open WebCMS">
    From the main menu, click **WebCMS**, then navigate to the **FAQ** section.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Add FAQ">
    Click the **Add FAQ** button to open the creation form.

    ![Add FAQ Button](/modules/cms-management/cms-faq-add-button.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Fill in the form">
    Complete the required fields — **Faq Category**, **Question**, **Answer**, and **Order** — in the Add FAQ form. Click **Add New Category** if the required category does not already exist.

    ![Add FAQ Form](/modules/cms-management/cms-faq-add-form.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Submit">
    Click **Submit** to create the FAQ.

    ![FAQ Added](/modules/cms-management/cms-faq-add-success.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The new FAQ is created and appears in the FAQ list, ready to be viewed on the website.

![FAQ List Updated](/modules/cms-management/cms-faq-success-list.png)

- The FAQ is published on the website's **FAQ** page under its category, for visitors to view.

![FAQ Page](/modules/cms-management/cms-faq-add-user-view.png)
