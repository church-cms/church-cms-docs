## Overview

The **Posts** page displays all posts published on the website, along with their category, date, and status. Administrators can review and manage posts from this page.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open WebCMS">
    From the main menu, click **WebCMS**, then select **Posts**.

    ![Posts Menu](/modules/cms-management/cms-post-menu.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="View Posts List">
    The Posts page will display all posts with their Title, Category, Date, and Status. Use the **All Statuses** and **All Categories** filters to narrow the list.

    ![Posts List](/modules/cms-management/cms-post-list.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- All posts are listed for the administrator to review and manage.
