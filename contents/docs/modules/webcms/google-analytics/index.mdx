## Overview

The **Google Analytics** page displays website traffic insights, including visitors and pageviews, total visitors and pageviews, top referrers, user types, and top browsers.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open WebCMS">
    From the main menu, click **WebCMS**, then select **Google Analytics**.

    ![Google Analytics Menu](/modules/cms-management/cms-google-analytics-menu.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="View Google Analytics">
    The Google Analytics page will display the **Visitors and Pageviews**, **Total Visitors and Pageviews**, **Top Referrers**, **User Types**, and **Top Browsers** data.

    ![Google Analytics List](/modules/cms-management/cms-google-analytics-list.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The website's Google Analytics insights are displayed for the administrator to review.
