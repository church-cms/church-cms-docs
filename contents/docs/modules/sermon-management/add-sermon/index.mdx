## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Sermons">
    From the main menu, click **Sermons**.

    ![Sermons Menu](/modules/sermon-management/sermon-menu.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Add Sermon">
    Click the **+ Add Sermon** button located at the top-right corner of the page.

    ![Add Sermon Button](/modules/sermon-management/add-sermon-button.png)
  </StepItem>
   <StepItem title="Enter the required details">
    A form will open. Fill in the following fields:
    - **Title** — Enter the sermon title.
    - **Description** — Enter a brief description of the sermon.
    - **Cover Image** *(required)* — Click **Pick from Media Library** to select an image. Once selected, the image preview will appear with options to **Change Image** or **Remove** it.

    ![Create Sermon Form](/modules/sermon-management/create-sermon-form1.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Enter the required details">

    ![Create Sermon Form](/modules/sermon-management/create-sermon-form.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Create">
    Click the **Create** button to save and create the sermon.
  </StepItem>

   <StepItem title="">

    ![Create Sermon Form](/modules/sermon-management/sermon-created.png)
  </StepItem>

    <StepItem title="User web">
      Click the **Sermons** List.
    ![Create Sermon Form](/modules/sermon-management/user-sermonlist.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>



## Outcome

- A new sermon will be created and listed in the Sermon section.
- The sermon will be published and visible with its title, author, and cover image.

<Note type="info">
  After creating a sermon, you can add chapters to it by editing the sermon from the list.
</Note>
