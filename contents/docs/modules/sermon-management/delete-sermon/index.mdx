---
title: How to Delete a Sermon
description: Step-by-step guide to deleting a sermon and all its chapters in ChurchCMS.
---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Sermons">
    From the main menu, click **Sermons**.

    ![Sermons Menu](/modules/sermon-management/sermon-menu.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Delete">
    In the sermon list, find the sermon you want to remove and click the **Delete** link in the Actions column.

    ![Delete Sermon Action](/modules/sermon-management/sermon-list-delete.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Confirm the deletion">
    A confirmation dialog will appear with the message:

    > *"Delete this sermon and all its chapters?"*

    Click **OK** to confirm and permanently delete the sermon, or click **Cancel** to abort.

    ![Delete Confirmation](/modules/sermon-management/delete-sermon-confirm.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The sermon and all its associated chapters will be permanently removed from the system.
- The sermon will no longer appear in the sermon list.

<Note type="warning">
  This action is irreversible. Deleting a sermon will also delete all its chapters and attached media files.
</Note>
