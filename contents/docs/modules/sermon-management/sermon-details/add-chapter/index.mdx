---
title: How to Add a Chapter to a Sermon
description: Step-by-step guide to adding a new chapter to an existing sermon in ChurchCMS.
---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Sermons">
    From the main menu, click **Sermons**.

    ![Sermons Menu](/modules/sermon-management/sermon-menu.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open the Sermon's Chapters page">
    In the sermon list, click the chapter count number under the **Chapters** column to open the sermon's details page.

    ![Sermon List](/modules/sermon-management/view-sermon-list.png)
  </StepItem>

  <StepItem title="Open the Sermon's Chapters page">
    In the sermon details list, click the chapter count number under the **Chapters** column to open the sermon's details page.

    ![Sermon List](/modules/sermon-management/sermon-details-list.png)
  </StepItem>

  <StepItem title="Click Add Chapter">
    Click the **+ Add Chapter** button at the top-right of the Chapters page.

    ![Add Chapter Button](/modules/sermon-management/sermon-details-list.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Fill in the chapter details">
    An **Add Chapter** dialog will appear. Fill in the following fields:

    - **Chapter Title** *(required)* — Enter the chapter title (e.g. *Part 1 — Introduction*).
    - **Publish Date** *(required)* — Select the publish date for the chapter.
    - **Video URL** *(optional)* — Enter a YouTube or video link for the chapter.
    - **Audio URL** *(optional)* — Enter a SoundCloud or audio link for the chapter.
    - **PDF / Doc** *(optional)* — Click **Choose File** to upload a PDF or document file.

    ![Add Chapter Form](/modules/sermon-management/add-chapter-form.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Submit">
    Click the **Submit** button to save the chapter.
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The new chapter will be added to the sermon and listed in the Chapters table.
- Attached media (video, audio, PDF) will be accessible from the chapter row.

<Note type="info">
  At least one media type (Video URL, Audio URL, or PDF) is recommended per chapter so members can access the sermon content.
</Note>
