---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Sermons">
    From the main menu, click **Sermons**.

    ![Sermons Menu](/modules/sermon-management/sermon-menu.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open the Sermon's Chapters page">
    In the sermon list, click the chapter count number under the **Chapters** column to open the sermon's details page.

    ![Sermon List](/modules/sermon-management/sermon-list.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Edit on the chapter">
    In the Chapters table, find the chapter you want to update and click the **Edit** button in the Actions column.

    ![Chapter Edit Button](/modules/sermon-management/chapter-edit-button.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Update the chapter details">
    The **Edit Chapter** dialog will open with the current details pre-filled. Update any of the following fields as needed:

    - **Chapter Title** *(required)* — Modify the chapter title.
    - **Publish Date** *(required)* — Change the publish date.
    - **Video URL** *(optional)* — Update the YouTube or video link.
    - **Audio URL** *(optional)* — Update the SoundCloud or audio link.
    - **PDF / Doc** *(optional)* — Click **Choose File** to replace the existing file. Leave blank to keep the current file.

    ![Edit Chapter Form](/modules/sermon-management/edit-chapter-form.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Update">
    Click the **Update** button to save the changes.
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The chapter details will be updated and reflected immediately in the Chapters table.

<Note type="info">
  Leave the **PDF / Doc** field blank if you do not want to replace the existing file.
</Note>
