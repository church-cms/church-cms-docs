## Overview

The **Rejected** tab displays prayer requests that have been reviewed and rejected by the administrator, typically due to inappropriate content or other reasons.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Prayer Board">
    From the main menu, click **Prayer Board**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Select Rejected tab">
    Click the **Rejected** tab to view all prayer requests that have been rejected.

    ![Rejected Prayer Requests](/modules/prayerboard-management/prayer-reject-list.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- All prayer requests with a **Rejected** status are listed. These requests will not be shown to other members.
