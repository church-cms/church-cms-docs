## Overview

The **Pending** tab displays prayer requests that have been submitted but not yet reviewed or acted upon by the administrator.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Prayer Board">
    From the main menu, click **Prayer Board**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Select Pending tab">
    Click the **Pending** tab to view all prayer requests awaiting review.

    ![Pending Prayer Requests](/modules/prayerboard-management/prayer-pending-list.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- All prayer requests with a **Pending** status are listed for the administrator to review and take action.
