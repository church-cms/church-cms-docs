## Overview

The **States** page displays all states available in the system, along with their associated country and status. Administrators can review and manage states from this page.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Master Data">
    From the main menu, click **Master Data**, then select **States**.

    ![States Menu](/modules/master-data/masterdata-state-menu.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="View States List">
    The States page will display all states with their Name, Country, and Status. Use the **All Countries** and **All Statuses** filters to narrow the list.

    ![States List](/modules/master-data/masterdata-state-list.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- All states are listed for the administrator to review and manage.
