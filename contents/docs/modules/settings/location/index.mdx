## Overview

The **Location Settings** page allows administrators to set the church's physical address and map location. This information can be displayed to members and visitors to help them find the church.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Settings">
    From the main menu, click **Settings**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Select Location">
    On the Settings page, click the **Location** tab from the left-hand list.

    ![Location Settings](/modules/settings/location-settings.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Enter Location Details">
    Fill in the church's address and location details such as street address, city, state, country, and map coordinates or embed link.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Save Changes">
    Click **Save Changes** to apply the location settings.
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The church's location details are saved and will be visible to members and visitors on the platform.
