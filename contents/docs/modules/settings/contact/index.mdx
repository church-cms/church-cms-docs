## Overview

The **Contact Settings** page allows administrators to update the church's contact information, including phone numbers, email addresses, and physical address. This information is displayed to members and visitors across the platform.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Settings">
    From the main menu, click **Settings**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Select Contact">
    On the Settings page, click the **Contact** tab from the left-hand list.

    ![Contact Settings](/modules/settings/contact-settings.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Update Contact Details">
    Fill in or update the required contact fields such as phone number, email address, and physical address.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Save Changes">
    Click **Save Changes** to apply the updates.
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The church's contact details are saved and displayed to members and visitors across the platform.
