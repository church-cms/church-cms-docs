## Overview

The **Social Media Settings** page allows administrators to add and manage the church's social media profile links. These links are typically displayed in the site footer or contact section for members and visitors to connect with the church online.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Settings">
    From the main menu, click **Settings**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Select Social Media">
    On the Settings page, click the **Social Media** tab from the left-hand list.

    ![Social Media Settings](/modules/settings/social-media-settings.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Enter Social Media Links">
    Fill in the URLs for the church's social media profiles, such as:

    - **Facebook**
    - **Instagram**
    - **Twitter / X**
    - **YouTube**
    - **LinkedIn**
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Save Changes">
    Click **Save Changes** to apply the social media links.
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The church's social media links are saved and displayed on the site for members and visitors to follow.
