## Overview

The **General Settings** page lets administrators configure the church's basic identity details — full name, short name, site logo, and favicon.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Settings">
    From the main menu, click **Settings**.

    ![Settings Menu](/modules/settings/settings-menu.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Select General">
    On the Settings page, click the **General** tab from the left-hand list.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Update General Settings">
    Configure the following fields:

    - **Church Full Name** — the complete name of the church.
    - **Church Short Name** — a short abbreviation for the church.
    - **Site Logo** — upload a logo image (360 × 90 px, 4:1 ratio, PNG or SVG with transparent background, max 2 MB).
    - **Favicon** — upload a favicon image (512 × 512 px, PNG only).

    ![General Settings](/modules/settings/general-settings.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Save Changes">
    Click **Save Changes** to apply the updates.
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The church's general identity details are updated and reflected across the platform.
