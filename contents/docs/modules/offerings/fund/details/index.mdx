## Overview

The **Fund Details** page displays the full information of a specific offering fund, including its configuration and associated donation records.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Offerings">
    From the main menu, click **Offerings**, then navigate to the **Fund** section.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click View">
    In the Fund list, click the **View** button on the fund you want to inspect.

    ![View Button](/modules/offerings/offering-fund-view-button.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Review Fund Details">
    The Fund Details page will display the full information for the selected fund.

    ![Fund Details](/modules/offerings/offering-fund-view-details.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The full details of the selected offering fund are displayed for the administrator to review.
