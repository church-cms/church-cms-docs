## Overview

The **Donation Details** page displays the full information of a specific donation record, including the donor details, amount, and transaction information.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Offerings">
    From the main menu, click **Offerings**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click View">
    In the Donation List, click the **View** button on the donation record you want to inspect.

    ![View Button](/modules/offerings/offering-donation-view-button.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Review Donation Details">
    The Donation Details page will display the full information for the selected donation record.

    ![Donation Details](/modules/offerings/offering-donation-details.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The full details of the selected donation record are displayed for the administrator to review.
