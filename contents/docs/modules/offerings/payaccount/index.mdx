## Overview

The **Pay Account** page displays all configured payment accounts used to receive offering donations. Administrators can review and manage the available payment accounts from this page.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Offerings">
    From the main menu, click **Offerings**.

    ![Offerings Menu](/modules/offerings/offering-payaccount-menu.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="View Pay Account List">
    The Pay Account list page will display all configured payment accounts available for receiving donations.

    ![Pay Account List](/modules/offerings/offering-payaccount-list.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- All configured payment accounts are listed for the administrator to review and manage.
