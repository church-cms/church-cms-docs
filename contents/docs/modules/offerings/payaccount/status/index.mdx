## Overview

The **Pay Account Status** feature allows administrators to activate or deactivate a payment account. This controls whether the account is available to receive offering donations.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Offerings">
    From the main menu, click **Offerings**, then navigate to the **Pay Account** section.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Status">
    In the Pay Account list, click the **Status** button on the account you want to update.

    ![Status Button](/modules/offerings/offering-payaccount-status-button.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Confirm Status Change">
    A confirmation dialog will appear. Confirm the action to toggle the account status between active and inactive.

    ![Status Change](/modules/offerings/offering-payaccount-status.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The payment account status is updated. Inactive accounts will not be available for receiving donations until reactivated.
