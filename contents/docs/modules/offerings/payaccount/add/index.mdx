## Overview

The **Add Pay Account** page allows administrators to configure a new payment account to receive offering donations from members.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Offerings">
    From the main menu, click **Offerings**, then navigate to the **Pay Account** section.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Add Pay Account">
    Click the **Add Pay Account** button to open the creation form.

    ![Add Pay Account Button](/modules/offerings/offering-payaccount-add-button.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Fill in the form">
    Complete the required fields in the Add Pay Account form.

    ![Add Pay Account Form](/modules/offerings/offering-payaccount-add-form.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Submit">
    Click **Submit** to save the new payment account.

    ![Pay Account Created](/modules/offerings/offering-payaccount-success.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The new payment account is created and appears in the Pay Account list, ready to receive offering donations.

![Pay Account List](/modules/offerings/offering-payaccount-list-success.png)
