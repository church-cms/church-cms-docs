## Overview

The **Delete Pay Account** feature allows administrators to permanently remove a payment account that is no longer needed.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Offerings">
    From the main menu, click **Offerings**, then navigate to the **Pay Account** section.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Delete">
    In the Pay Account list, click the **Delete** button on the account you want to remove.

    ![Delete Button](/modules/offerings/offering-payaccount-delete-button.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Confirm Deletion">
    A confirmation dialog will appear. Click **OK** to permanently delete the payment account.

    ![Delete Confirmation](/modules/offerings/offering-payaccount-delete.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The payment account is permanently removed and will no longer appear in the Pay Account list.

<Note type="info">
  This action cannot be undone. Ensure the account is no longer in use before deleting.
</Note>
