# Guest Menu Options

The guest profile provides several quick-action options accessible from the menu. Each option performs a specific account management task.

![Guest Menu Options](/modules/guest/guest-menu-option.png)

---

## Deactivate

Deactivates the guest account to prevent login access.

1. Click **Deactivate**.
2. A confirmation dialog will appear.
3. Click **OK** to confirm.

![Deactivate Guest](/modules/guest/guest-menu-deactivate.png)

---

## Verify Email

Sends an email verification request to the guest's registered email address.

1. Click **Verify Email**.
2. A verification email will be sent to the guest.

![Verify Email](/modules/guest/guest-menu-verifyemail.png)

---

## Newsletter

Manages the guest's newsletter subscription status.

1. Click **Newsletter**.
2. Toggle the guest's subscription as required.

![Newsletter](/modules/guest/guest-menu-newsletter.png)

---

## Message

Sends a direct message to the guest.

1. Click **Message**.
2. Compose and send the message from the dialog.

![Message Guest](/modules/guest/guest-menu-message.png)

---

## Exit Guest

Converts or removes the guest status from this profile.

1. Click **Exit Guest**.
2. Confirm the action when prompted.

![Exit Guest](/modules/guest/guest-menu-exitguest.png)
