# Guest Detail

## Overview

The **Guest Detail** page displays the complete profile information of a guest, including their personal and contact details.

---

## Accessing Guest Details

From the **Guest List**, click the **View** button on the guest's row to open their detail page.

![Guest View Button](/modules/guest/guest-view-button.png)

---

## Guest Profile

The guest detail page shows the full profile of the selected guest.
