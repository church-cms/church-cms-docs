# Guest Management

## Overview

The **Guest** section lists all individuals who have visited or registered as guests of the church. Administrators can view guest details and manage guest records from this page.

---

## Accessing the Guest List

From the main menu, navigate to **Users → Guests**.

![Guest Menu](/modules/guest/guest-menu.png)

---

## Guest List

The guest list displays all registered guests with their key details at a glance.

![Guest List](/modules/guest/Guest-list.png)

---

## Search & Reset

- Use the **Search** bar to find a guest by name.
- Click **Reset** to clear the search and return to the full list.
