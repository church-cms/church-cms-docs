---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Quotes / Bible Verse">
    From the main menu, click **Quotes / Bible Verse**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click View">
    In the list, click the **View** button on the entry you want to view.

    ![View Button](/modules/quotes-bibleverse/quotes-view-button.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="View Quote / Bible Verse">
    The full details of the quote or Bible verse will be displayed.

    ![View Quote](/modules/quotes-bibleverse/view-quote.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>
