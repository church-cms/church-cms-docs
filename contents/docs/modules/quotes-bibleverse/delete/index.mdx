---
title: How to Delete a Quote / Bible Verse
description: Guide to permanently deleting a quote or Bible verse in ChurchCMS.
---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Quotes / Bible Verse">
    From the main menu, click **Quotes / Bible Verse**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Delete">
    In the list, click the **Delete** button on the entry you want to remove.

    ![Delete Button](/modules/quotes-bibleverse/quote-delete-button.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Confirm Deletion">
    A confirmation dialog will appear asking you to confirm the deletion. Click **OK** to permanently delete the entry.

    ![Delete Confirmation](/modules/quotes-bibleverse/quote-delete.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The quote or Bible verse will be permanently removed from the system and will no longer appear in the list.

<Note type="info">
  This action cannot be undone. Ensure you want to permanently delete the entry before confirming.
</Note>
