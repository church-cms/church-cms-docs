## Overview

The **Mail Queue** page displays emails that are scheduled to be sent through campaigns and newsletters, including subscriber and mailing list details.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Email Blaster Settings">
    From the main menu, click **Email Blaster**, then click **Settings**, and select **Mail Queues**.

    ![Mail Queue Menu](/modules/email-blaster/email-settings-mail-queue-menu.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="View Mail Queue">
    The Mail Queue page will display all queued emails with their Email, Subscriber, Campaign, Mailing List, and Scheduled At details.

    ![Mail Queue List](/modules/email-blaster/email-settings-mail-queue-List.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- All queued mail records are displayed for the administrator to review.
