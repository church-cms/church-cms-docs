## Overview

The **Mails Delivered** page displays the delivery status of emails sent through campaigns and newsletters, including subscriber, mailing list, and tracking details.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Email Blaster Settings">
    From the main menu, click **Email Blaster**, then click **Settings**, and select **Mails Delivered**.

    ![Mails Delivered Menu](/modules/email-blaster/email-settings-mail-delivered-menu.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="View Mails Delivered">
    The Mails Delivered page will display all delivered emails with their Email, Subscriber, Mailing List, Campaign, Scheduled At, Fired At, Read, and Clicks details.

    ![Mails Delivered List](/modules/email-blaster/email-settings-mail-delivered-list.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- All delivered mail records are displayed for the administrator to review.
