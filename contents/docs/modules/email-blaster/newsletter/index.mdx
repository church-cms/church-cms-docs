## Overview

The **Send Newsletter** page allows administrators to compose and send a newsletter email to all subscribers or a selected mailing list.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Email Blaster">
    From the main menu, click **Email Blaster**, then click **Send Newsletter**.

    ![Send Newsletter Menu](/modules/email-blaster/email-newsletter-menu.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Fill in the form">
    Select the recipients from the **To** dropdown, enter a **Subject**, and compose the **Message**.

    ![Newsletter Form](/modules/email-blaster/email-newsletter-form.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Submit">
    Click **Submit** to send the newsletter.
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The newsletter is sent successfully to the selected recipients.

![Newsletter Sent Successfully](/modules/email-blaster/email-newsletter-success.png)
