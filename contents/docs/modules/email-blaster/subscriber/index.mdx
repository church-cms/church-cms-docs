## Overview

The **Subscriber List** page displays all members and guests who have subscribed to receive email communications. Administrators can review and manage subscribers from this page.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Email Blaster">
    From the main menu, click **Email Blaster**, then navigate to the **Subscriber** section.

    ![Subscriber Menu](/modules/email-blaster/email-subscriber-menu.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="View Subscriber List">
    The Subscriber list page will display all subscribed members and guests with their details.

    ![Subscriber List](/modules/email-blaster/email-subscriber-list.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- All email subscribers are listed for the administrator to review and manage.
