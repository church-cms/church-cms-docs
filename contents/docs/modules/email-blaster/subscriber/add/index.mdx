## Overview

The **Add Subscriber** page allows administrators to manually add a new subscriber to the email list.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Email Blaster">
    From the main menu, click **Email Blaster**, then navigate to the **Subscriber** section.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Add Subscriber">
    Click the **Add Subscriber** button to open the creation form.

    ![Add Subscriber Button](/modules/email-blaster/email-subscriber-add-button.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Fill in the form and Submit">
    Complete the required fields and click **Submit** to add the subscriber.
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The new subscriber is added and appears in the Subscriber list.

![Subscriber Added](/modules/email-blaster/email-subscriber-success.png)
