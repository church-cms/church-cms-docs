## Overview

The **Campaign List** page displays all email campaigns created for sending bulk communications to members and guests. Administrators can review and manage campaigns from this page.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Email Blaster">
    From the main menu, click **Email Blaster**, then navigate to the **Campaign** section.

    ![Campaign Menu](/modules/email-blaster/emai-campaign-menu.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="View Campaign List">
    The Campaign list page will display all created email campaigns with their status and details.

    ![Campaign List](/modules/email-blaster/emai-campaign-list.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- All email campaigns are listed for the administrator to review and manage.
