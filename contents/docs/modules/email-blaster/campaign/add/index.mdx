## Overview

The **Add Campaign** page allows administrators to create a new email campaign to send bulk communications to members and guests.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Email Blaster">
    From the main menu, click **Email Blaster**, then navigate to the **Campaign** section.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Add Campaign">
    Click the **Add Campaign** button to open the creation form.

    ![Add Campaign Button](/modules/email-blaster/emai-campaign-add-button.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Fill in the form">
    Complete the required fields in the Add Campaign form.

    ![Add Campaign Form](/modules/email-blaster/emai-campaign-add-form.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Submit">
    Click **Submit** to create the campaign.

    ![Campaign Created](/modules/email-blaster/emai-campaign-add-success.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The new campaign is created and appears in the Campaign list, ready to be sent to members and guests.

![Campaign List](/modules/email-blaster/emai-campaign-success-list.png)
