## Overview

Administrators can view the full details of a submitted contact request, including the requester's contact information, query, submission date, and browser details.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Contact Requests">
    From the main menu, click **Contact Requests**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click View">
    On the Contacts list page, click **View** next to a contact request.

    ![Contact Requests View Button](/modules/contact-requests/contact-view-button.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="View Contact Details">
    The Contact Details page displays the **Name**, **Mobile Number**, **Email**, **Query**, **Submitted On** date, and **Browser Details** of the request.

    ![Contact Details](/modules/contact-requests/contact-details.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The full details of the selected contact request are displayed for the administrator to review.
