title: Add Members to a Group
description: Guide to adding and managing members within a group in ChurchCMS.
---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Groups">
    From the main menu, click **Groups** and open the group you want to manage.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Add Members">
    Click the **Add Members** button to open the member selection form.

    ![Add Members Button](/modules/groups/add-members-button.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Search and select members">
    Use the search field to find members by name. Select the members you want to add to the group from the search results.

    ![Search Members](/modules/groups/search-members.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Assign a Member Role">
    Select a role for the member — such as **Admin** or **Member** — to define their level of access within the group.

    ![Member Group Admin](/modules/groups/member-group-admin.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click Submit">
    Click **Submit** to add the selected members to the group.
  </StepItem>

  <StepItem title="Group members">
    Use Group members

    ![Search Members](/modules/groups/group-details.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The selected members will be added to the group and listed in the group's member list.
- Each member's role within the group determines their access and permissions.

<Note type="info">
  Members assigned the **Admin** role within a group can manage group content and other members.
</Note>
