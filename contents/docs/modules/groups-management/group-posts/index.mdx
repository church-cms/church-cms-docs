## Overview

The **Group Posts** section displays all messages and posts shared within a group. Group admins and members with the appropriate role can post messages that are visible to all group members.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Groups">
    From the main menu, click **Groups** and open the group you want to view.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Click View Posts">
    Click the **View** button on the group row to open the group posts page.

    ![Group Post View Button](/modules/groups/group-post-viewbutton.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="View Group Posts">
    The group posts page will display all messages and posts shared within the group. Group admins and members can post messages visible to all group members.

    ![Group Post View](/modules/groups/group-post-view.png)
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Who Can Post

| Role | Can Post |
|------|----------|
| **Group Admin** | Yes — can post messages and manage posts. |
| **Member** | Yes — can post messages within the group. |

<Note type="info">
  Only members who belong to the group can view and post messages. Group admins can moderate posts within the group.
</Note>
