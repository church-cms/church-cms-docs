---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

<Step>
  <StepItem title="Login to the system">
    Login to the system using **Admin credentials**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Open Reports">
    From the main menu, click **Reports**.
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Go to the Membership section">
    On the Reports page, locate the **Membership** card.

    ![Report List](/modules/reports/reports-list.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="Download the required report">
    Click the download icon next to the report you need:

    - **Active Members** — downloads a CSV list of all active members.
    - **Guest Members** — downloads a CSV list of all guest members.
    - **Suspended / Expired Members** — downloads a CSV list of suspended or expired members.

    ![Download Membership CSV](/modules/reports/report-download-member-csv.png)
  </StepItem>
  <StepItem title="File downloaded">
    The CSV file is downloaded to your device and also appears in the **Recent download history** panel.
  </StepItem>
</Step>

## Outcome

- The exported CSV (e.g. `ActiveMembers_<date>.csv`, `GuestMembers_<date>.csv`) is saved to your device.
- The download also appears in the **Recent download history** list for quick access later.

<Note type="info">
  Each Membership report can be downloaded independently — click only the download icon for the report you need.
</Note>
